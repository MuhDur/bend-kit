# Changelog

Older changes are in the git history. Each package's version is `<pkg>/VERSION`. On merge to `main`, CI publishes `bend-kit-<package>@<VERSION>` unless that version is already on the hub.

The kit is in alpha. A breaking change bumps the second number (`0.3.0.0` to `0.4.0.0`). Do not keep a compatibility shim.

When you change `<pkg>.bend` or `effs/`, raise `VERSION` and add a line here for that package.

## Unreleased

- CI: `scripts/pins.sh` fails on a `scripts/pins.allow` line that no import uses, and when a `VERSION` rises it lists the siblings still on the old version.
- CI: `scripts/pins.sh` fails a pull request whose changed packages import a sibling at a version other than its `VERSION`, or by hash; `scripts/pins.allow` lists the waves still waiting on a publish. `webhooks` builds natively, so two versions of one C effect cannot slip back in.

Breaking: `Http` and `Hairpin` types come from those versions.
- `gateway` 0.2.0.0: imports `cache` 0.2.0.0 and `collections` 0.1.3.0. Its one-key cache proofs use the published `order_string_refl` in place of a local proof about `key.cmp`. Breaking: `Gate` holds a `cache` 0.2.0.0 `Cache`.
- `cache` 0.2.0.0, `sigv4` 0.5.0.1: order `String` keys with `Order.string` from `bend-kit-collections@0.1.3.0`. `cache` proves that `start`, `complete`, `invalidate`, and `evict` on one key leave every other key's slot as it was, and that every operation keeps the slots a search tree. Breaking for `cache`: `key.cmp` is gone, and `Cache` holds a `collections` 0.1.3.0 `OMap`.
- `collections` 0.1.3.0: `order.bend` adds `Order.string`, a code-point order on strings that does not rebuild them. Proved: it is reflexive, antisymmetric, transitive, and EQ means equal strings, so it meets the `OMap` laws. `LAWS.bend` and `PROOF.bend` now publish with the package, so dependent packages can cite the `OMap` and `Order` laws.
- `hairpin` 0.7.0.0: `open.as` follows the client's redirect mode, presents its certificate, and sends and stores cookies through its jar, as `request.as` does. The client's jar comes back with the stream's `Set-Cookie` fields. Breaking: imports `http` 0.34.0.0, so `Http` types come from that version.
- `gateway` 0.1.0.0: new package. One gate composes `authz`, `resources`, and `cache`: authorize, take one unit, read the cache, and on a miss start a fetch that `finish` completes and gives back. Proved: a denied request reserves nothing and starts no fetch; a full pool answers Busy and changes nothing; hits, misses, and every upstream result (found, missing, failure, open breaker) give back exactly the unit they took; and an older fetch that answers late does not change the cache. `app.bend` is a Camber app with one guarded route that fetches through `hairpin` with a retry budget and a circuit breaker.
- `http` 0.34.0.0: `open.how(mode, cert, jar, method, url, headers, body, ms)` and `open.raw.how` open a stream with the redirect mode, client certificate, and cookie jar of `fetch`, and return the jar beside the stream. They reuse `fetch`'s redirect policy and jar logic. `ModeError` fails on a redirect, `ModeManual` returns the redirect as the stream, the certificate follows only same-origin hops, and each hop sends and stores cookies. `open`, `open.with`, `open.raw`, and `open.raw.with` keep following redirects with no certificate and no jar. Breaking: `stream.hops` takes the mode and a `StreamHops` state and returns the jar beside the stream; `open.with.enc` is now `open.how.enc`.
- `camber` 0.9.0.0: the owner keeps lent resources in a `bend-kit-resources@0.1.0.0` pool. `Counts.in_use` is the pool's reserved count. A `Returned` with no matching `Submit` stops the owner instead of clamping `in_use` at zero. Breaking: `OwnerStep` carries a `Ledger`, not `Counts`.

Breaking: `Http`, `Time`, `Json`, and `Bytes` types come from the converged versions.

- `cbor` 0.2.0.0, `csv` 0.2.0.0, `encoding` 0.4.0.0, `files` 0.2.0.0, `hash` 0.2.0.0, `http2` 0.3.0.0, `json` 0.6.0.0, `multipart` 0.2.0.0, `process` 0.3.0.0, `property` 0.3.0.0, `regex` 0.7.0.0, `time` 0.2.0.0, `postgres` 0.2.0.0, `redis` 0.2.0.0: import `bend-kit-bytes@0.3.2.0` by name, in place of the 0.3.0.0 hash pin or 0.3.1.0. `regex` imports `bend-kit-unicode@0.1.0.0` by name. Breaking: `Bytes` in their APIs is the 0.3.2.0 type. Their READMEs are checked against the code.
- `runner` 0.1.0.0: new package. A durable job runner from `journal`, `scheduler`, `resources`, and `retry`. Each queue transition is one fsync'd journal record, and `open` replays them, then requeues the tickets that a crash left running. A `resources` pool bounds the attempts that run at once; failed attempts requeue at retry's wait, and a breaker per target defers new attempts while it is open. Proved: replay of any committed prefix equals the live state, a completed or cancelled ticket is never selected again (after recovery too), each attempt holds one pool unit and gives it back once, and a requeue waits retry's wait. `example.bend` is a webhook outbox through `webhooks` and `hairpin`.
- `llm` 0.5.0.0: `stream` opens through `Hairpin.open.as`, so a stream open shares the retry budget, backoff, and circuit breaker that `send` uses. Its own retry loop is gone. Breaking: imports `hairpin` 0.5.1.0, so `Hairpin` types come from that version, and a denied stream open is `ErrNet{ErrDenied{...}}`.
- `hairpin` 0.5.1.0: `open.as` opens a streamed response under the same budget, deadline, backoff, and circuit breaker as `request.as`. A refused stream closes when the next attempt is admitted; the last one comes back open.
- `hairpin` 0.5.0.0: reads the monotonic clock with `Time.mono.ms` from `bend-kit-time@0.1.3.0`. Breaking: `Hairpin.now.ms` is gone; call `Time.mono.ms()` from that version. `Http` `Instant`s still come from `time` 0.1.2.1.
- `scheduler` 0.2.0.0: `live.bend` and the benchmark read `Time.mono.ms` and `Time.mono.us` from `bend-kit-time@0.1.3.0`. Breaking: `clock.bend` (`Clock.now`, `Clock.now_us`, `Clock.micros`) is gone.
- `llm`, `webhooks`, `sigv4`, `jwt`, and `oauth2` 0.4.0.0: import `hairpin` 0.4.0.0, and `llm`, `webhooks`, and `sigv4` import `Retry` from `bend-kit-retry@0.1.0.0`. Breaking: `Hairpin` and `Retry` types come from those versions, so a `bend-kit-retry` budget passes straight to `Webhooks.send.in`.
- `time` 0.1.3.0: `mono.ms` and `mono.us` read the monotonic clock as a `Nat` of milliseconds or microseconds, through the pure `Duration.to_ms_nat` and `Duration.to_us_nat`.
- `hairpin` 0.4.0.0: imports the retry policy from `bend-kit-retry@0.1.0.0`. Breaking: `bend-kit-hairpin@0.4.0.0/retry.bend` is gone; import `bend-kit-retry@0.1.0.0/retry.bend`, and `Retry` types come from that package. The retry laws and proofs moved there too.
- `retry` 0.1.0.0: new package. Hairpin's retry policy (attempt budget, deadline, backoff, circuit breaker) and its proofs, with no HTTP imports.
- `cache` 0.1.0.0: cache validity for keyed values. Fetches take per-key generations; a completion lands only at or above the key's floor, then raises it. Invalidation voids fetches in flight, eviction keeps generations, and entries expire by TTL or negative TTL from the fetch start. Proved over any sequence of operations on a key: no expired read, stale completions stay stale, an older fetch cannot overwrite a newer one, and eviction changes only the current entry.
- `authz` 0.1.0.0: new package. Pure authorization policies with deny-overrides, default deny, intersection, and fail-closed missing context, with proved composition laws. `check.bend` guards a Camber route with it.
- `journal` 0.1.0.0: an append-only journal. Each record is one frame with a checked length and a CRC-32. Appends are fsync'd before they answer. `open` cuts a torn tail and refuses a corrupt frame. Proved on a list model: round trip, exact committed prefix after a torn append, corruption that a later tail cannot hide, order kept with nothing invented, and replay equal to a fold over the recovered records.

- `hairpin` 0.2.0.0: a proven retry policy in `retry.bend`: a shared attempt budget across nested layers (`Hairpin.request.in`), operation deadlines (`Hairpin.deadline`), bounded jittered backoff, and a circuit breaker (`Hairpin.circuit`). A 64-bit length of 2^32 or more is rejected even with the largest max.
